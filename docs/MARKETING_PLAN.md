# DailyVibe Marketing Master Plan

## Phase 1: The Engagement (Immediate)
**Goal:** Warm up the audience who reacted to your "Teaser" post.
*   **Action:** Reply to every comment/like on your Facebook teaser.
*   **Script:** "Thanks! I'll send you the link as soon as it's live! 🚀"
*   **Why:** Facebook's algorithm bumps posts with active comments to the top of friends' feeds.

---

## Phase 2: Asset Prep (While Waiting for Review)
**Goal:** Have all marketing materials ready so you can launch instantly upon approval.

### 1. Build the Website (`dailyvibe.fun`)
*   **Status:** In Progress (See `WEBSITE_PLAN.md`).
*   **Why:** A professional link (`dailyvibe.fun`) converts better than a raw App Store link.

### 2. Create the 15-Second Video Ad
**Tools:** Canva, TikTok, or Instagram Reels.
**Audio:** Upbeat, lofi hip-hop or energetic instrumental.

**Script:**
*   **[0:00-0:03] Problem:**
    *   *Visual:* Blurred screenshot of a messy notes app or confused person.
    *   *Text Overlay:* "Struggling to stay consistent?"
*   **[0:03-0:06] Solution:**
    *   *Visual:* Clear, high-res screen recording of DailyVibe (Dark Mode) checking off a habit.
    *   *Text Overlay:* "Meet DailyVibe. Simple. Distraction-free."
*   **[0:06-0:10] Features:**
    *   *Visual:* Quick cuts of: Streak counter -> Dark Mode -> No Ads banner.
    *   *Text Overlay:* "Build streaks. No ads. Just vibes."
*   **[0:10-0:15] Call to Action:**
    *   *Visual:* The "Download Banner" (App Store + Play Store logos).
    *   *Text Overlay:* "Download FREE now at dailyvibe.fun"

---

## Phase 3: The "Go Live" (Launch Day)
**Goal:** Get 10-20 downloads and 5+ ratings in the first hour.

### 1. The Announcement Post (Facebook/Socials)
**Image:** Your "App Launch" banner (Phone with "Available Now" text).
**Caption:**
> The wait is over! 🚀 **DailyVibe** is now available on iOS and Android.
>
> Start building better habits today (for free).
>
> ✨ Clean, Dark Mode Design
> ✨ No Ads or Tracking
> ✨ simple Streak Tracking
>
> 📲 **Download here:** https://dailyvibe.fun
>
> P.S. If you download it, please leave a quick 5-star rating—it helps me out huge!

### 2. The "Soft Launch" Direct Message (VIP List)
**Action:** Send this to 5-10 close friends/family via Messenger/WhatsApp. Do NOT mass spam; send individually.
**Script:**
> "Hey! My app DailyVibe just went live. Would you do me a huge favor? Could you download it and leave a quick 5-star rating? It really helps the app store algorithm find it. Thank you so much!"

### 3. Facebook Story Update
*   **Visual:** Screenshot of the app on the App Store page.
*   **Sticker:** "Link" sticker pointing to `dailyvibe.fun`.
*   **Text:** "WE ARE LIVE! 🚨 Download now."

---

## Phase 4: Momentum (Week 1)
**Goal:** Keep the app visible without being annoying.
*   **Day 3 Post:** Share a screenshot of *your own* streak in the app.
    *   *Caption:* "3 days strong on my 'Read 10 pages' habit. Who else is building a streak? Post yours!"
*   **The "Review" Share:** If a friend leaves a nice review, screenshot it and post it to your Story.
    *   *Text:* "Thanks [Name]! glad you're loving the dark mode."
